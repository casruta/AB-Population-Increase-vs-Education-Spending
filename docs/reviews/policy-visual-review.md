# Independent policy and visual review

Reviewer: Astra policy/visual agent, independent of the presentation author. Review scope: generated README, chart renderer, canonical series definitions, methodology and derived publication tables. The separate evidence review covers source admissibility.

## Review criteria and observations

- The brief opens with a dated observation, why the distinction between financial measures matters, and a decision request. It does not infer funding adequacy, causality or institutional efficiency.
- K–12 and post-secondary have equal panel area and consistent visual hierarchy. Separate scales and headcount/FLE units are disclosed; the text explicitly rejects a cross-sector ranking.
- Each infographic section has a finding, policy question, limitations and linked data. The plans-and-delivery evidence gap is preferable to unsupported or zero-valued variance bars.
- The post-secondary expense panel is restricted to three universities. It is not substituted for the 20-institution grant boundary.
- Independently read the exported observations: the latest supported amounts are K–12 revenue $8,169/headcount, K–12 expense $9,021/headcount, post-secondary grants $8,440/FLE and three-university expenses $14,817/FLE, all rounded constant-2012 dollars. Matched years are 8, 8, 8 and 2 respectively. The comparable three-university pair gives a 0.7866% decrease in real expenses/FLE and a 7.6284% increase in nominal expenses on the selected definition.
- Inspected all three full-size PNGs and independent 820-pixel-width copies. Main values, period labels and units are legible and neither panel is clipped. The author enlarged the scope subtitles and explanatory text; refreshed 820-pixel previews were inspected after the fixes. Colour is supplementary to panel labels and does not encode the only identifying information.

## Corrections requested from the presentation author

1. Replace the inaccurate statement that isolated dots always mark breaks: a valid two-year pair is also shown as separate dots.
2. Annotate material accounting/reporting changes inside the figures, including why the post-secondary grant panel ends before the latest available financial actuals.
3. Label the K–12 delivery card as provincial revenue, and identify the three-university expense subset within its delivery card.
4. Describe the nominal change as expenses on the selected definition, not unadjusted total expenses.
5. Enlarge/wrap the fine scope and explanatory text for GitHub reading width.

## Verification of corrections

All five requested changes were implemented by the presentation author and independently verified in the renderer, generated README and refreshed images. The final grant figure explicitly names the withheld periods as 2023–24 and 2024–25, avoiding an ambiguous range beside the starting-year axis. Numerical claims continue to agree with the derived table. The renderer connects only runs of at least three consecutive observations sharing a comparability segment; the comparable two-year university observations remain separate dots with truthful explanatory text.
