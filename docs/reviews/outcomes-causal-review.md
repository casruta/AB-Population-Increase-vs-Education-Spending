## Verified snapshot evidence

The official Alberta Education Annual Report 2024–25, printed pages 23 and 27–29, reports five-year completion of 86.2% in 2019–20 and 87.1% in 2023–24, peaking at 88.6% in 2021–22. The newest five-year cohort entered Grade 10 in 2019–20. The report explains pandemic examination exemptions and weighting changes that may affect completion.

Grade 9 mathematics acceptable-standard attainment was 54.3% in 2022–23 and 52.7% in 2023–24. Participation rose from 83.9% to 84.9%. The ministry excludes some 2021–22 security-breach data and warns about pandemic comparability. Grade 9 language arts is a weighted composite across courses. This is a snapshot, not confirmation that no newer outcome release exists.

## Critique and revision loop

1. Evidence agent records government source, page/table, reporting date, observation period, denominator, coverage, status and limitations for each claim.
2. Calculation agent independently recomputes headline figures and checks matched populations and inflation periods.
3. Outcomes reviewer challenges cohort timing, participation, curriculum changes and equity coverage.
4. Causal reviewer labels each proposed conclusion as observed, plausible mechanism or causal evidence; rejects unsupported causal attribution.
5. Policy reviewer challenges cost, feasibility, delivery capacity and the proposed measurable benefit.
6. Editor resolves each material finding in the report or documents why it remains uncertain. Reviewers inspect revised claims and diagrams. Acceptance requires no unresolved material defect; documented evidence limitations remain visible.
7. New audited spending, finalized enrolment or outcome releases reopen affected claims and restart the relevant checks.

Each pass records the issue, evidence, requested change, response and final disposition. A completed first review is not approval of a later draft. Stop revising when acceptance gates pass; do not promise indefinite improvement or imply uncertainty can be eliminated.

## Policy evidence standard

Protect general purchasing power while separately costing changing student needs. Target documented learning gaps, track the delivery of staff and supports alongside dollars, and evaluate phased interventions against a credible comparison group. Each recommendation should identify a responsible organization, baseline, service-delivery measure, student outcome, cost basis and review date. Province-level correlations do not justify a quantitative estimate of funding's causal return.
