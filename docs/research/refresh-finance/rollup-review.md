## Comparative-year limitations

Footprints for Learning (27) explicitly says its PSAS-restated 2024 comparative statements and schedules 1–8 are **unaudited**, because the auditor was not engaged to report on them. It is a newly entering authority in this roster and should not be used to support an audited same-source comparative claim.

Horizon (44) has an unmodified opinion with emphasis on a prior-period revenue-recognition correction: note 22 reduces 2024 investment income and receivables/surplus by $78,899. Red Deer Catholic (64) has an unmodified opinion with an emphasis that 2024 comparative information was restated (note 3). Clearview (16) emphasizes estimates rather than an audit qualification; its policy note describes prospective revenue/intangible adoption and P3 retrospective application without restating prior comparatives. These observations demonstrate why an unqualified current opinion does not establish an unchanged cross-year accounting basis.

The 79-common-authority cohort can be useful research evidence if its audited/restated comparatives, matched enrolment denominator and changing scope are separately disclosed. It remains a cohort comparison, not a full province trend extension. Current-year standalone extraction is supported despite these comparative limitations.
