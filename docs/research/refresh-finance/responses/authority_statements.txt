K to 12 education financial statements



						

  Combined statements, summary reports, and school authority audited financial statements.


											

				

			
		

	


	
	

		
  




	
	

		
  


    
Skip to On this page

  
        

    
  

  
  

    
  
  

    
  
Combined statements
Combined financial statements provide a roll-up provincial total, by school jurisdiction compiled from information reported within the individual statements and schedules.
2023/2024 combined financial statements
2022/2023 combined financial statements
2021/2022 combined financial statements
2020/2021 combined financial statements
2019/2020 combined financial statements
Summary reports
Summary reports list summary revenue, expense and surplus amounts for each public, separate, francophone and charter school authority in the province for the school year.
2023/2024 audited financial statements – summary reports
2022/2023 audited financial statements - summary reports
2021/2022 audited financial statements – summary reports
2020/2021 audited financial statements – summary reports
2019/2020 audited financial statements – summary reports
School authority reports
Individual audited financial statements for each public, separate, francophone and charter school authority in the province for the school year.
2024/2025 school authority audited financial statements
2023/2024 school authority audited financial statements
2022/2023 school authority audited financial statements
2021/2022 school authority audited financial statements
2020/2021 school authority audited financial statements
