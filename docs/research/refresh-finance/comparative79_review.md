# Independent review of the 79 continuing-authority comparison

**Recommendation: publish only a narrowly described research comparison of reported expenses**, with the source, cohort and accounting caveats adjacent to the result. The mechanically reconstructed result is **−1.3222% in reported expenses excluding amortization per matched enrolled student, inflation-adjusted, for 79 continuing authorities, 2023-24 to 2024-25**. It does not establish a province-wide change, a stable-accounting-basis change in real resources, or a continuous historical trend. Do not label every comparative as independently audited merely because the current-year report is audited.

## Accounting/restatement interpretation

Clearview PDFp31 and Southern Francophonep29 describe adoption effectiveSeptember1,2023, before both fiscal years used here; both state no purchased-intangible opening impact/financial-statement change. These passages alone do not demonstrate a newly introduced2024-25 break. However, they do not justify equating expense accounting with all educational resources. Calgary Catholicp18 says P3 adoption occurred2023-24 and an accumulated-depreciation adjustment was reflected2024-25. Edmontonp18 reports a prior opening P3 carrying-value adjustment of$29,585,572. These are specific reasons to keep the result as reported-accounting expense evidence.

Reviewed substantive restatements: Horizonp38 corrects2024 investment income/receivables/surplus by−$78,899, without identifying an expense adjustment; Red Deer Catholicp27 reclassifies$732,301 between invested-in-TCA and operating reserve, says no material operations impact; Peace Riverp36 increases TCA and spent-deferred-capital by$10,184,506; Prairie Rosep32 reclassifies five expense categories whose adjustments sum0. Restated labels on supplemental fee schedules or on a current-year budget do not by themselves establish a prior expense accounting break. Do not infer that every restated flag changes the numerator, or that an unmodified audit makes accounting bases identical.

Use wording such as: “Using2024-25 statements and their2023-24 comparatives, reported expenses excluding amortization per matched student were1.32% lower in inflation-adjusted terms among79 continuing authorities. The comparison excludes entering/exiting authorities and is subject to accounting and audit limitations.” Avoid “real education resources fell1.32%” or “Alberta education spending fell1.32%.” Capitalized purchased intangibles, P3 changes and other accounting classification effects would require a separately evidenced bridge to support the broader resource claim.
