        2026-29 Business Plan and Supplementary Performance
        Measures Backgrounder



        Measures used in the business plan are a part of the ministry’s overall performance measurement framework. The measures
        are selected to assess the success of the education system in meeting the outcomes published in the business plan.

        Together with the business plan measures, the supplementary measures in the summary table reflect a comprehensive
        assessment of the education system.

        This comprehensive performance measurement framework contains three types of measures: administrative, assessment and
        survey. These three different types of measures rely on different methods of data collection, which broadly capture all aspects
        of the education system than would be the case if the framework relied on a single method of data collection.

        The measures in the framework cover six diverse categories. These categories are listed below with the number of associated
        measures shown in brackets:

          A.   Student Learning: (23 measures and 1 indicator)

          B.   Opportunity to Learn: (4 measures)

          C.   Involvement: (3 measures)

          D.   Teaching: (1 measure)

          E.   Leadership and Governance: (4 measures)

          F.   Facilities: (1 measure)

        All measures are reliable, valid, comparable over time, and follow a consistent and robust methodology. These different types
        of measures help in understanding the performance of the provincial education system.
