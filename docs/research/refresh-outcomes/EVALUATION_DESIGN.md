# Concrete next decision: test additional Grades 7–9 mathematics support

The verified signal is a decline in Grade 9 mathematics attainment, with increased
participation, alongside large instructional groups in part of the reported class
snapshot. Diploma attainment improves, so this is a targeted junior-high problem
to investigate rather than evidence of universal educational deterioration.
Neither aggregate signal identifies a cause or a successful intervention.

**Decision to evaluate:** whether additional mathematics instruction and support
time at documented-need schools improves learning enough to justify continued
funding and staff capacity. A pilot can assess delivery feasibility before expansion.

1. **Confirm need and ability to deliver.** Authorities combine diagnostic
   mathematics assessments, attendance and service waits with classroom records,
   school-specific growth and actual staff vacancies. Choose clear eligibility
   criteria; use EAL or recent arrival only where a documented educational need
   makes them relevant. Obtain local staffing FTE, actual teaching/support hours
   and school/authority spending. Neither class size nor a needs label alone
   specifies the right staffing model.
2. **Specify the intervention.** Record the amount and type of additional
   instruction, group sizes, staff qualifications, delivery frequency and
   implementation dates. Distinguish money allocated, staff recruited and support
   actually delivered. Monitor whether schools can fill positions before committing
   to a scale-up that relies on unavailable staff.
3. **Create a comparison before delivery.** Where feasible, allocate the timing of
   equally eligible schools by a transparent random phased rollout. Otherwise,
   select comparable schools with similar prior mathematics outcomes, participation,
   attendance, cohort needs and enrolment trends. State that a comparison using
   existing trends can still suffer confounding or regression to the mean.
4. **Track learning and participation together.** Define the primary outcome before
   results arrive. Use comparable curriculum-aligned diagnostic scores at baseline
   and after a full school year, with PAT acceptable-standard attainment and
   participation as complementary outcomes. Follow the same pupils where locally
   governed linkage is possible, report moves and missing assessments, and compare
   like grade/curriculum cohorts. Do not substitute higher participation for better
   learning, or interpret enrolled-attainment complements as writer failures.
5. **Assess implementation, equity and costs.** Record delivered support hours,
   vacancies, waiting times, attendance and actual cost per participating pupil.
   Examine results for relevant needs and student groups without treating identities
   as deficits. Report uncertainty and the costs of an implementable improvement;
   do not promise a causal return on spending based on provincial associations.
6. **Make the continuation decision.** Decide in advance what size of improvement,
   level of delivery and absence of harmful tradeoffs would support continued
   funding. Evidence of no delivery is different from evidence that a delivered
   intervention did not help. Use the comparison and uncertainty to determine
   whether to continue, redesign or expand.

For completion, the appropriate horizon is longer. The same 2019–20 entering
Grade 10 cohort gained 6.4 percentage points between its three- and five-year
completion observations overall, and 12.7 points among self-identified First
Nations, Métis and Inuit students. These descriptive gains warrant tracking
continued pathways and re-engagement, with community-designed supports, rather
than treating three-year noncompletion as permanent failure. They do not establish
which service or expenditure produced the later completion.

The 2024–25 PAT observation is a baseline predating the 2025–26 funding rise in
the earlier financial comparison. The November 2025 class snapshot is early in
2025–26 and does not measure that year's final staffing or outcomes. Budget 2026
hiring commitments concern 2026–27 to 2028–29. Keep these exposure, delivery and
outcome dates separate when designing follow-up.
