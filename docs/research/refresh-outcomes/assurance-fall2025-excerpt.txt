What's New for Fall 2025?
This section outlines any changes to the data, calculations or measures compared to the Spring 2025 release.

Data Updates

Grade 6 Provincial Achievement Test (PAT) results:

In the 2024/25 school year, the status of Grade 6 Provincial Achievement Tests (PATs) administered were:
        ·    New Curriculum:
                o English Language Arts and Literature 6, Mathematics 6

         ·     Optional Implementation:
                  o Social Studies 6

         ·     Not Administered:
                  o French Language Arts 6 année, Français 6 année, Science 6


Grade 6 aggregate Provincial Achievement Test (PAT) results are not available:
       · Aggregate reporting is only possible for tests that have achievement standards and are administered to
          all students across the province. No Grade 6 tests administered in the 2024/25 school year met these
          criteria.


Grade 9 Provincial Achievement Test (PAT) results:
       ·   New year of aggregate Grade 9 Provincial Achievement Tests (PATs) results.


Diploma Examination results:
      ·   The New year of Diploma Examination (DIP) results.

Achievement Evaluation

Achievement evaluation is based upon a comparison of Current Year data to a set of standards which remain consistent over time. The Standards are calculated by taking the three-year average of
baseline data for each measure across all school jurisdictions and calculating the 5th, 25th, 75th and 95th percentiles. Once calculated, these standards remain in place from year to year to allow for
consistent planning and evaluation.
